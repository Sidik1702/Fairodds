FairOdds turns a forecasting model into a market maker with a public conscience. The model commits every prediction to the ForecastRegistry before its pm-AMM market opens, seeds the market at its own probability instead of 50/50, and after resolution the program itself computes the Brier score. No cherry-picking, no retro-editing — the track record below is read straight from Solana devnet.

How it works

1

Engine

A kernel-weighted comparables model estimates P(YES) for each SOL/BTC threshold question — backtested honestly against naive momentum and the flat base rate.

2

Commit

Before trading opens, the keeper writes the prediction — market, probability, strike, model version, hash — into a ForecastRegistry PDA. One record per market: it can never be edited or re-committed.

3

Seed

The pm-AMM market is created with its initial price set to the model's probability (6200 bps = 62%), not a flat 50/50 — the market opens where the model stands.

4

Resolve

At expiry the outcome is read from the reference price. (On devnet today this is a documented Coinbase-at-expiry read — the legacy Pyth devnet feed is stale, and the record says so.)

5

Attest

The keeper attests the outcome and the program itself computes the Brier score in-program, stored next to the original commitment. Anyone can recompute the track record from chain data alone.
