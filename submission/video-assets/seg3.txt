Five steps. One: the forecast engine — a kernel-weighted comparables model — estimates the probability that SOL closes above a strike in twenty-four hours. Two: before trading opens, the keeper commits the prediction — market, probability, strike, model version, hash — into a ForecastRegistry record. One record per market; it can never be edited. Three: the pm-AMM market is created, seeded at the model's probability. Four: at expiry, the outcome is read from the reference price. Five: the keeper attests — and the program itself computes the Brier score.
