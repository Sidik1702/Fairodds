Now the honest part — our backtest. Over six thousand three hundred walk-forward forecasts on a hundred and eighty days of SOL and BTC prices, the model's Brier score is zero point one zero seven — twenty-seven percent better than naive momentum, and fifty-seven percent better than a coin flip. But in this sample — one long downtrend — it only ties the unconditional base rate, and we say so in the report. Version one's claim is narrower and defensible: calibrated, fair seeds, with every future forecast graded in public, on-chain.
